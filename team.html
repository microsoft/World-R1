World-R1 Team

World-R1 is a collaboration between Microsoft Research and Zhejiang University on reinforcement learning for 3D-consistent text-to-video generation.

Research Groups

Microsoft Research

MSR Asia - Shanghai

MSR Asia - Shanghai develops advanced algorithms and systems for real-world intelligent applications, spanning reinforcement learning and decision making, computer vision, language generation, video systems, cloud gaming infrastructure, efficient cloud-edge AI computing, and emerging World Model technologies.

  • Reinforcement learning, decision making, and multimodal intelligence.
  • Video systems and visual computing for temporal modeling and scene understanding.
  • Efficient AI infrastructure for scalable foundation-model and World Model workloads.
Visit MSR Asia - Shanghai

Zhejiang University

ZIP Lab

ZIP Lab focuses on next-generation efficient AI computing, large language models, multimodal foundation models, and World Model research, exploring how capable models can learn, reason, and generate under practical compute constraints.

  • Efficient training, adaptation, and deployment for large-scale foundation models.
  • Large language models, multimodal foundation models, and generative World Models.
  • Model-system co-design for practical and scalable generative AI research.
